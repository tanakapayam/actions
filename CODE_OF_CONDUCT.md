# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is incorporated here by reference.

In short: be kind and be useful. Criticize ideas and code, not people. Assume good faith, and say what would help. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately by opening a [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/tanakapayam/actions/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct": only the maintainer can read it. Reports are handled promptly and in confidence, and the reporter is not named without their agreement.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who do not follow this code, as the Contributor Covenant describes.
